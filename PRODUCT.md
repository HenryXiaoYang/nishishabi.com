# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Chinese internet users who receive a shared link or screenshot from a friend/colleague/groupchat. The scenario is casual, social, meme-driven. Primary emotion: mild amusement → shared laughter → impulse to retaliate/spread. The joke is delivered through design: the more deadpan-official the certificate looks, the funnier the insult.

## Product Purpose

A single-purpose viral meme site: <name>.你是傻逼.com renders a dynamically-generated certificate declaring "<name>, 你是傻逼！" Anyone can create one by entering a name on the home page; the resulting subdomain URL is the share artifact (sent via WeChat, QQ, iMessage, etc). The loop is: receive link → laugh → create one for someone else → repeat. Design is the product: there's no storage, no accounts, no features beyond the joke itself.

Success = high share-through rate + screenshot spread + sustained viral transmission. The cleaner and more modern the design, the sharper the punchline.

## Brand Personality

**Deadpan modern**. Restrained, clean, precise — "seriously stating an absurd thing" is the comedic mechanism. Not garish, not emoji-laden, not overstimulating. A hint of mischief underneath the polish (坏笑反骨), but never crossing into crude or low-quality.

Three words: **modern · subversive · precise**.

## Anti-references

1. **The original saturated-yellow elementary-school certificate aesthetic**: loud orange borders, red stroke text, gear-badge clipart, busy flourishes. That was the joke's first draft; we've moved on.
2. **Generic AI slop**: cream/sand body bg + gold accents + uppercase eyebrows + identical card grids. The current warm-neutral OKLCH palette is fine because it's tied to the brand seed and applied with restraint, not as a category reflex.
3. **Over-designed or maximalist**: excessive decoration, animation, clutter. The joke is the content; the design should be invisible infrastructure that makes the punchline land.

## Design Principles

1. **Deadpan execution**: The more official and restrained the visual treatment, the funnier the insult. Design straight, let the content do the comedy.
2. **Instant legibility**: Certificate pages must read clearly at a glance (for screenshots) and on any screen size. Typography, contrast, and layout serve clarity first.
3. **Frictionless creation loop**: Home → input name → certificate → share → repeat. Every step should feel instant and obvious; no cognitive load, no feature bloat.
4. **Modern restraint over loud decoration**: Clean, precise, professional. Not "fun" in the decorative sense; fun emerges from the tension between the polished design and the absurd declaration.
5. **Preserve the share artifact**: The subdomain URL is the meme. Don't gate it, track it invasively, or require sign-up. Anonymous, instant, viral-friendly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, keyboard navigable, reduced-motion alternatives. This is a casual meme site targeting Chinese social-media virality; AAA or full i18n is over-investment. The current cream-bg + warm-ink palette already hits AA; maintain that floor.
